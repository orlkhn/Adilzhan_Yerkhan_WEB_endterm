NIGHTSHIFT — SOURCES AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS
WEB Technologies 1 (Front End) — Endterm, 2026

PROJECT
NightShift is a fictional student concept for choosing and planning evening activities.
All eight venues, prices, durations and recommendations are illustrative; these are not verified businesses.
There is no backend, payment integration, or real booking feature. Saved items exist only in the visitor's browser.
